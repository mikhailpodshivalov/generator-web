Three sound engines, one instrument

AI, FM, and samples are equal first-class sources — mix them freely across the eight slots.

Neural AI + Harmony Conductor

Lead lines come from neural voices trained on a licensed MIDI corpus and tuned per genre. An AI harmony conductor then sets the chord movement bar by bar and drives the basslines to match, so melody and bass stay locked in the same key. Every result is seed-driven — recall or reroll it exactly.

Digitone-class FM Synth

A 4-operator FM TONE engine with 16 algorithms, per-operator waveforms and modes, polyphonic leads, feedback, and macro control. Browse the FM preset bank, save your own, or bounce any patch straight to a sample.

Full Sample Engine

Every slot can play samples: a 260-sound factory bundle, or drop in your own sample packs and randomize patterns with them. Multisample per track, per-step p-locks, envelopes, filters, and a 3-lane LFO on top.

Random, sequencer and instrument set

Seed-driven generation, full step sequencing, and deep sound control across every slot.

Deep per-track control & modulation

Go past the grid: shape timing, groove, and movement independently on every track.

Your own sample packs

Point MG at a folder of your samples and they show up alongside the factory bundle — loadable on any slot and fully part of the random engine, so patterns get generated with your own sounds.

L / D / T — per-track timing

Set each track's Length, play Direction (forward, reverse, ping-pong, random), and time scale independently. Different lengths per track give you polyrhythms and evolving loops for free.

G — groove table

An M8-style per-track groove table for shuffle and micro-timing, with classic swing folded in. Push and pull individual steps to give each part its own feel.

LFO — 3-lane modulation

Three modulation lanes per track with many destinations, tempo-synced or free-running. Automate cutoff, pitch, FM macros, sample parameters, and more.

GEN — auto-gen engine

Turn a track into a live note generator that keeps composing over your set Length, following the AI harmony conductor so the movement always fits the track.

Song Mode

Chain patterns into a full song with repeat-aware steps and playback-follow, or generate a complete arrangement with one button, then edit it down to the detail.

Play it like hardware

Random is optional. Underneath is a full Elektron-grade sequencer you can drive entirely by hand.

Write tracks from scratch

No randomization required. Place trigs and notes step by step, dial the synth, and build a whole track by hand — the same hands-on flow as a hardware Elektron box.

Elektron-style trig menu

Every step carries conditional trigs (X:Y cycle, FILL, PRE, 1ST, NEI and their inverses), micro-timing nudge, retrig / ratchet with its own rate, trig probability, slide, accent, note, length, and parameter locks.

Octatrack-style scene morph

Store a BASE and a SCENE snapshot of your parameters, then crossfade between them with a single morph control — manually or on an auto-morph shape for constantly evolving motion.

Perform mode

Live PADS and note-repeat: hold to roll at any retrig rate, play sample slices from the keys, and hit FILL to drop in your FILL-conditioned trigs on the fly.

Up to 16 tracks

Resample & source freedom

Resample a track, the full mix, or a region into a fresh sample, then play it back like any other. Drop any FM patch or any sample onto any slot or single step.

FAQ

Does MG use AI?

Yes — melodic lead lines are generated by neural voices trained on a licensed MIDI corpus, tuned per genre. An AI harmony conductor sets the chords for each bar and steers the basslines to match, keeping the whole track in key. No cloud, no prompts: generation runs fully offline on your machine.

Can I use my own samples?

Yes. MG ships with a factory sample bundle, and you can point it at your own sample folders. Your sounds load on any slot and take part in random pattern generation just like the built-in ones.

What is the FM synth?

A 4-operator FM TONE engine in the spirit of the Elektron Digitone: 16 algorithms, per-operator waveforms and modes, polyphonic leads, a preset bank, and bounce-to-sample. It sits next to the sample engine on every slot.
